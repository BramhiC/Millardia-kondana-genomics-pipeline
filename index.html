Millardia kondana Whole Genome Pipeline

Reference-guided whole genome analysis for a non-model, high-elevation rodent restricted to isolated lateritic plateaus in the northern Western Ghats.

Background

Millardia kondana is known from four geographically isolated plateau systems: Sinhagad, Rajgad, Torna and Raireshwar. Limited dispersal among these plateaus may have resulted in population isolation, reduced gene flow, loss of genetic diversity and increased inbreeding. The pipeline presented here is designed for whole-genome resequencing data from multiple populations of a non-model species lacking a species-specific reference genome.

Objectives

  1. Estimate current and historical effective population size
  2. Assess gene flow and genetic differentiation among populations
  3. Quantify genetic diversity within and among populations
  4. Estimate genomic inbreeding and runs of homozygosity
