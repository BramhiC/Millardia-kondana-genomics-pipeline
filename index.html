Millardia kondana Whole Genome Pipeline

Reference-guided whole genome analysis of four isolated plateau populations: Sinhagad, Rajgad, Torna and Raireshwar.

Study Design

  • M. meltada: 10 samples
  • Total: 50 whole-genome samples

Objectives

  1. Current and historical effective population size
  2. Gene flow and genetic differentiation among plateau populations
  3. Genetic diversity
  4. Inbreeding coefficient and runs of homozygosity
